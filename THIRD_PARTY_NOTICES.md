# Third-party notices

Distraction Killer Launcher bundles website preset lists under
`app/src/main/assets/presets/`. Most of them are hand-curated for this project
and released into the public domain. Two are derived from third-party data,
described below. The lists are generated by `tools/build_presets.py`.

## StevenBlack/hosts (adult and gambling presets)

The `adult` and `gambling` presets are derived from the "porn" and "gambling"
extensions of the StevenBlack/hosts project:

- https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StevenBlack/hosts/master/extensions/porn/sinfonietta/hosts
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StevenBlack/hosts/master/extensions/gambling/sinfonietta/hosts

Within StevenBlack/hosts, those two extensions are sourced from Sinfonietta's
hostfiles (https://github.com/Sinfonietta/hostfiles). The data has been
transformed for this app: hosts entries were normalised, a leading `www.` was
removed, and every host was collapsed to its registrable domain (eTLD+1), so
the bundled files are not a verbatim copy of the upstream lists.

## Public Suffix List (build time only)

`tools/build_presets.py` uses the Public Suffix List
(https://publicsuffix.org/, https://github.com/publicsuffix/list) to collapse
hostnames to their registrable domains. The list is downloaded into
`tools/.cache/` when the presets are generated and is not bundled with the
app. It is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0
(https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/).
